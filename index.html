    Teams differ on guarantees

    Average guaranteed amounts differ significantly by signing team (p = .0455). The Rams, Chargers, Browns, Eagles, and Chiefs spend the most.

    Draft round matters, with exceptions

    First-round picks have longer careers on average (p < .0001). Quarterbacks and linebackers are the exception: second-round picks lasted longest.

    04 · RETAIL PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS · TEAM OF 3

    Deciding who a $7 sales call should go to.

    54% → 22%

    misclassification: gradient boosting beat the baseline so that about 4 in 5 campaign calls would reach customers likely to buy a membership.

    Question
    Which customers should a paid-call membership campaign target?
    My role
    Built 3 of the team’s 9 advanced models and contributed to preprocessing and the final recommendation.
    Key decision
    Turned a 0–5 interest score into likely/unlikely to match the real choice: call or don’t call.

    SAS Enterprise Miner · Gradient boosting · Neural networks · Decision trees

    What I still use Judge a model by what its mistakes cost, not just its accuracy.

    Drivers, method & limits ↗
    View full size ↗

    What drove the prediction

    Recency of last purchase, interest in accessories, and email opens and clicks. Customers who engage recently are the ones who convert.

    Limits

    The model predicts survey interest, not observed purchases. The next step would be measuring real conversion before rollout.

    Ask me about trading false positives for false negatives ↗
